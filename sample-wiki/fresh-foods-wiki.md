# Allergen policy
All bakery items are made in a facility that also processes peanuts and tree nuts. Labels list every major allergen. If a customer reports an allergic reaction, offer a full refund and tell them our Quality Manager, Dana, will call within 24 hours.

# Pest control and store cleanliness
Every store has weekly professional pest control. Any sighting of rodents or insects must be reported to the store manager immediately; the affected aisle is closed and inspected the same day.

# Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. Store credit is issued instantly.
